Why Shangbo Uses Only Virgin PA66 for Nylon Cable Ties

By Austin Zhang, Shangbo Plastics

Most nylon cable ties look the same in a photo. Where they differ is the resin inside them. At Shangbo Plastics, our standard nylon cable ties are moulded from 100% virgin PA66, and we don't blend recycled nylon into them. This note explains why, and how a buyer can check it.

What "virgin PA66" means

Virgin PA66 is nylon 66 resin that has not been moulded before. Recycled or regrind nylon has already been melted at least once, and every melt cycle shortens the polymer chains a little. The material still moulds, and the tie still looks fine on arrival, but it has less margin left for cold, heat and long service outdoors.

Why we stay with virgin resin

A cable tie fails at its weakest point, usually the pawl or the neck where the strap meets the head. Those thin sections are where a shorter-chain material shows up first. For buyers who resell to electricians, solar installers or panel builders, one batch of brittle ties costs more in returns and reputation than the saving on resin.

So our standard ties use virgin PA66 from resin rated UL 94 V-2, on our 40 injection moulding machines in Bozhou, China. If a buyer needs a different material, we also make polypropylene ties, a flame-retardant PA66 V-0 series and stainless steel ties in 201, 304 and 316.

How a buyer can check it

  1. Which resin and which grade? A real answer names the material, not "high quality nylon".
  2. Can you send the material certificate? We provide the resin certificate of analysis on request.
  3. What happens on the reorder? We keep retained samples from your first shipment with no time limit, so a later order can be compared with the original.

There is a longer write-up on recycled versus virgin nylon cable ties on our website if you want the detail.

Test results

Our nylon ties were assessed by TÜV Rheinland to EN IEC 62275. The declared loop tensile strengths on that data form are 80 N for 2.5 mm, 180 N for 3.6 mm, 220 N for 4.8 mm, 530 N for 7.6 mm and 800 N for 9.0 mm ties.
